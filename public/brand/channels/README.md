# Channel marks

`google-search.svg` is the unmodified [Google Favicon 2025 vectorization from Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Google_Favicon_2025.svg), a CC0-dedicated vector reconstruction by Designism, Liquinoid, and YeBoy371 of Google's gradient G. Google identifies the gradient G as its [Search icon](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/company-announcements/gradient-g-logo-design/). The Google mark remains subject to trademark rights. Search is labeled separately as a Google Ads campaign.

`google-ads.svg` is the unmodified [Google Ads icon from Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Google_Ads_icon.svg), attributed there to Google and described as public-domain geometry subject to trademark restrictions. It is displayed to identify the Google PMAX channel.

`google-discover.svg` is the unmodified [Google Discover icon from Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Google_Discover.svg), created by [KezBrit](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:KezBrit) and distributed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); no changes were made. This is the Discover logo, used as a visual identifier for Demand Gen at the user's request, not a separate official Demand Gen logo. Google trademarks remain subject to their owners' rights.

The Meta and Reddit SVGs are unmodified files from the [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) collection, used as single-color masks in the associated brand colors. Simple Icons is distributed under CC0; brand marks remain subject to their owners' trademark rights.

The Instagram gradient is the original [Instagram logo 2022](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Instagram_logo_2022.svg) from Wikimedia Commons. Commons describes it as public-domain geometry and notes that it is trademarked. The Klaviyo image is the unmodified 192px icon published on [Klaviyo's official website](https://www.klaviyo.com/icons/icon-192x192.png). All marks are shown only to identify the corresponding channels.

The locally retained rocket and image type symbols are [Google Material icons](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons), distributed under the Apache 2.0 license. They are interface symbols, not separate Google product logos.
