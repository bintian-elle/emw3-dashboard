# EDM Insights

本文件仅用于 EDM Dashboard Insights。以下六页 PDF 原文是主要分析提示词，完整保留全部章节、问题、benchmark 和输出要求；后续诊断补充用于提高分析准确度。根据请求语言作答，按当前报表周期和对比周期应用周报要求。

请对本周 EDM 渠道表现进行系统分析。分析的核心目的不是简单复述数据，而是从数据中识别
真正有价值的 Insights：

● 哪些地方可以优化？ 问题可能来自哪里？下一步应该如何调整？

1. Overall Insights 整体洞察

● 本周 EDM 整体表现是增长、持平还是下降？

● 是否存在值得提前关注的风险？
● 基于以上发现，下周最值得采取的行动是什么？

3. Subscribers 订阅用户
我想得到的信息：
判断本周 Email + SMS 的用户增长是否健康，并识别用户增长主要来自哪里。
需要分析的数据：
● Total New Subscribers
● New Email Subscribers
● New SMS Subscribers
● WoW / 近几周 Subscriber Growth
● 当前是否处于大促期
Benchmark：
● 大促期间：约 600 New Subscribers / Week
● 非大促期间：约 300–400 New Subscribers / Week
重点分析：
● 本周新增 Subscribers 是否达到正常水平？
● Email 和 SMS 分别贡献了多少新增？
● 与上周/上上周相比发生了什么变化？
● 是否存在异常增长或下降？
● Subscriber Growth 是否健康、可持续？
注意：大促期与非大促期的数据不能直接机械比较。
如果上周处于大促、本周恢复日常，本周 Subscribers 下降可能属于正常现象，不应直接判断为
表现变差。

## 补充诊断与结构化输出

Analyze this EDM dashboard's reporting period and comparison period. Use the supplied performance intelligence, relevant Bluevua project context and available project sources to produce an analytical diagnosis. Answer in the requested language.

## Select meaningful stories

A metric change by itself is not an insight. Every selected insight must connect multiple pieces of evidence and explain a relationship, driver, contradiction, funnel-stage change, concentration or business implication. Exclude insights that merely restate a KPI increase or decrease.

Generate candidate stories, then select the most valuable based on business impact, evidence strength, actionability and non-obviousness. Aim for 3–5 substantive stories when supported; prefer fewer strong stories over filler. Consolidate observations that describe the same underlying story.

## EDM diagnostic discipline

- Compare reach versus revenue, engagement versus conversion, orders versus AOV, Campaign versus Flow and Email versus SMS when relevant.
- Diagnose the Email funnel: Recipients → Delivered → Open → Click → Conversion → Revenue. Identify the largest meaningful deterioration and distinguish scale from efficiency.
- Respect the actual channel measurement model. SMS has no opens; keep its funnel and denominators separate from Email.
- Distinguish activity and composition changes from performance. Current-only and previous-only campaigns/messages represent changed activity, not a measured performance decline.
- Separate volume, engagement, conversion, AOV, mix and denominator effects. Use the supplied deterministic decomposition; explain offsetting positive and negative effects.
- Check contradictions and counterfactuals. For example, recipients rising while revenue falls does not support lower reach as the explanation.
- Assess revenue-loss concentration using the share of all negative revenue deltas, including top-one/top-three contributions and supplied concentration thresholds. Do not confuse it with net revenue change or total revenue concentration.
- Trace important drivers from EDM → Campaign/Flow → specific Campaign/Flow → Message → funnel metrics, with contribution evidence.
- Separate observation, supported interpretation, hypothesis and next investigation. Correlation alone does not establish causation.
- Preserve periods, attribution, units, currencies and comparable baselines. Explain missing or mismatched evidence rather than assuming missing observations are zero.
- Tie each action to a diagnosed driver, business implication and supporting evidence. When the cause remains uncertain, propose a concrete investigation.

## Evidence and output

For report metrics, cite the available metric paths, values, periods and units so readers can verify claims. For business context, cite the sources actually consulted and their dates. Distinguish a project summary from an original source that was retrieved for this analysis.

Produce the dashboard's structured Insights result: headline, executive_summary, performance_status, key_insights and recommended_actions. Each insight includes title, observation, interpretation, supporting_evidence, driver, business_implication, next_step and confidence. Confidence should reflect evidence strength.
