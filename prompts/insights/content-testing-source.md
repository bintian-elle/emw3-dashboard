## Overview

Tab: `t.97goxl6uv4su`

Overview
背景信息
Overview 主要总结 Content Testing 截至目前的整体表现，包括 Paid Channels 和 Organic Channels。重点展示整体投入、触达、ROAS，以及表现最好的 Messaging Angles。

1. Overall Performance: Total Spend、Total Reach、Overall ROAS（排除 Organic Channels，Reach 注意跨渠道重复计算）。

3. Creative Testing: 总共测试了多少组素材，包括 Google Search 的文字、Meta 和 Reddit 的图片及视频等。
4. Best Performing Messaging: 哪个 Messaging Angle 表现最好 (CTR)？是否有跨渠道表现较好的 Messaging？
5. Organic Performance: 简单总结 Social Media 和 EDM 的表现。
希望 AI 能具备的能力：
基于以上数据，自动生成一段简洁的 Executive Summary，不仅总结 Spend、Reach、ROAS 等基本表现，还能识别表现最好的 Channel 和 Messaging Angle，提炼关键 Insights，并给出下一步优化建议。
具体 Creative 的文字、视觉内容及 Video Hook 等深入分析，放在各个 Channel 的独立页面，不需要在 Overview 展开。
注意： 各渠道上线时间不同，AI 应根据实际投放周期分析，避免直接比较不同测试周期的表现。

* 挖掘新 Insights： 发现数据中值得关注的现象，例如 CTR 高但 CVR 低，或 Spend 增加但 Revenue 没有同步增长。
* 提出优化建议： 根据数据推荐下一步值得测试的 Messaging Direction，以及可能的预算调整机会。

Background
Social Media focuses on testing different Content Formats and Topics to understand audience interests and engagement.
Currently, our content strategy focuses on two Messaging Directions:
* Better You Can Feel
* Protection That Matters
The main Content Formats being tested include:
* Video: Trend / Visual Comparison / Step-by-Step / Close-ups
* Carousel: Educational
We will provide links to individual social media posts. We expect AI to analyze both the post content and associated performance data to identify which content formats drive stronger reach, engagement, and follower growth.
Key Questions We Want to Answer
1. Which individual posts perform best?
* Metrics: Views, Reach, Likes, Comments, Shares, Saves, Follows
* Which posts generate the highest views and reach?
* Which posts drive the most shares, saves, and new followers?
* Are there posts with high views but relatively low engagement?
* Which posts have successful elements worth replicating in future content?
2. Which Content Formats perform best? (Primary Testing Focus)
* Metrics: Views, Reach, Engagement Rate, Shares, Saves, Follows
* Video vs. Carousel: Which format generates stronger reach and engagement?
* Which content styles perform better: Trend, Educational, Visual Comparison, or Step-by-Step?
* Which formats are more effective at driving shares, saves, and follower growth?
3. Which Content Topics / Messaging Directions resonate most with the audience?
* Metrics: Views, Reach, Likes, Comments, Shares, Saves, Engagement Rate
* Which topics perform best under Better You Can Feel and Protection That Matters?
* Which topics generate stronger engagement: Product Demonstrations, Educational Content, Family Wellness, or Lifestyle?
* Does the same Messaging Direction perform differently across Content Formats?
4. How does Post / Format Performance change over time?
* Metrics: Views, Reach, Engagement Rate, Shares, Saves, Follows (WoW / MoM)
* Which Content Formats consistently perform well?
* Which Topics are gaining or losing engagement?
* Are there any emerging content trends worth exploring further?
5. What should we optimize next? (Opportunities)
* Metrics: Overall performance, with a focus on Engagement Rate, Shares, Saves, Follows, and Reach
* Which Content Formats should we prioritize or publish more frequently?
* Which Topics should we continue exploring?
* Which Content Format + Topic combinations should we test next?
2. Expected AI Capabilities

* Format Comparison: Automatically compare Videos, Carousels, and different content presentation styles to identify the most effective formats.
* Topic / Messaging Analysis: Compare engagement performance across different Content Topics and Messaging Directions.
* Engagement Insights: Identify meaningful patterns, such as posts generating high views but relatively few shares or saves.
* Trend Analysis: Track performance changes across Posts, Topics, and Formats over time.
* Optimization Recommendations: Recommend Content Formats, Topics, and presentation styles to prioritize in future content planning.

Tab: `t.kvdu1fcodt07`

背景信息
EDM 主要通过 RO vs. Pitcher 的内容测试，了解用户对不同 Messaging 和 Content 的兴趣。
目前每个月测试 1 封 Email，每封 Email 仅选择 1 个 Module 进行测试。
希望 AI 能结合测试 Module 的内容及 Click Rate，分析用户对该内容的点击兴趣，并为后续 Content Optimization 提供建议。
我们要得到哪些信息
1. 测试 Module 的点击表现如何？
* Metrics: Module Click Rate
* 该 Module 的 Click Rate 是多少？
希望 AI 能具备的能力
基础能力： Module Click Analysis
* Module Performance Analysis： 自动分析指定测试 Module 的 Click Rate。
* Content Recognition： 识别 Module 中的主要 Messaging、产品卖点及 CTA。
* Performance Insights： 结合 Module 内容与 Click Rate，提炼值得关注的发现。
* Optimization Recommendations： 针对 Module 的 Headline、Visual、Copy 或 CTA，提出下一轮测试建议。
* AI Summary： 自动生成一段简洁的分析，突出 Module Click Rate、内容观察及具体优化建议。
