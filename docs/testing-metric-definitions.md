# Testing metric definitions

- Overview Total ROAS sums Google, Meta and selected Reddit paid conversion value and spend before division. It does not average per-ad/channel ROAS, and excludes EDM revenue because no matching EDM advertising spend is present. Total Revenue still includes EDM revenue. The ROAS card shows numerator/denominator and keeps its total during channel hover.
- Overview CTR is advertising clicks / advertising impressions across Google Search, Google Demand Gen, selected Meta campaigns and configured Reddit ads. PMAX is excluded from both numerator and denominator using explicit channel metadata; missing impressions in an included channel still make CTR unavailable. Google period and daily CTR use the same non-PMAX scope and the legend says "excl. PMAX". EDM remains a separate unique-clicks/Delivered email rate and never contributes to overall advertising CTR, even when Delivered is positive. A zero Delivered denominator shows a dash with "Delivered 0"; positive delivery is marked "email only". Ratios are recomputed from raw totals, never averaged. Financial, revenue, purchase, ROAS and reach scopes are unchanged.
- Total Reach sums available Google campaign `unique_users`, Meta campaign reach and Reddit campaign reach. These are campaign sums, not cross-campaign/platform deduplicated totals. Unique Reach shows the sum of channel values, with per-channel legends; the EDM contribution is its API-deduplicated recipient count. At the user’s request these methodology notes are not shown in the UI. EDM Delivered is included in Total Reach. EDM Unique Reach uses the Received Email metric’s `unique` aggregation filtered to the currently selected test Campaign IDs, cached privately for six hours. Klaviyo returns unique counts per month; ranges with multiple nonzero monthly buckets are unavailable rather than incorrectly summed. Google reach uses one shared period-level query for the three Search campaigns, privately cached for six hours; it never sums daily unique counts. Overlapping concluded test reach is restricted to its lifetime. No Total Impressions card is added.
- Meta reach is read at campaign, ad-set and ad levels directly from period-level Insights. Campaign reach is never summed from creatives/ad sets. Cost / reach means USD spend divided by unique people reached, with up to four decimals; it is not CPM or cost per 1,000 people. Missing reach/zero denominator renders a dash.
- The extra Meta campaign aggregate UI is removed; ad-group summaries omit Hook rate. Creative-level Hook rate remains. Video detection uses expanded creative metadata (video_id, object_type, asset-feed video assets, story video metadata), with numbered names such as Video1 as a fallback. Video previews use Meta's official preview iframe.

Local verification: four videos and three images identified from the current Meta campaign; metric aggregation tests cover unequal spend/impression weights and zero denominators.

Business Performance preserves the original selected-period cards and appends daily line charts beneath their compact white-background legends and existing ratio details. Additive cards keep their total distribution bars and selected share. CTR, ROAS and CVR show no distribution bar or share percentage: a denominator share must not be presented as a click/conversion rate. Extra Daily badges and repeated metric-description captions are omitted. The layout retains three columns followed by two wider ratio cards on wide screens, two columns on medium screens and one on phones. Hover or keyboard arrows reveal the date and daily channel values; hover/focus/click on a legend highlights its series without changing the headline total. Legend values remain selected-period totals. Revenue/orders/CTR/ROAS use daily raw inputs with the existing scopes; conversion rate averages the same asset entities as the headline (Google Search groups, expanded Google rows, Meta/Reddit ads and EDM variants). Asset trends count distinct entities with delivery on each date, while the headline remains selected-period inventory. Daily unique reach is queried directly and never summed to recreate period reach. Google daily reach is unavailable in the current testing-entity source. Missing channels or denominators remain gaps; daily charts load separately from headline cards and independently report source failures. Klaviyo uses UTC event dates, matching its existing event-time totals.

Content Testing navigation separates Google Search, Google PMAX and Google Demand Gen at the same level as Meta, Reddit and EDM. Only explicitly confirmed creative tests are included; PMAX and Demand Gen remain empty until confirmed. The Brand_AbsTopPageTest placement experiment retains its stored definition and snapshots and remains visible under Concluded Test → Google Search; it is excluded only from active content-performance listings and totals via testScope=placement.

## Social Media (local implementation)

`/bluevua/testing/social` is an independent, authenticated Instagram post performance page. Its fixed ten-post selection is in `lib/instagram-posts.ts`. Overview shows a separately labeled lifetime Social summary; Social does not contribute to selected-period Business Performance totals, active-test counts, or Concluded Test navigation. Uses the existing server-only META_ACCESS_TOKEN, Graph v25.0, the Bluevua Page's connected Instagram account, and canonical permalink matching (including /reel/).

Views, reach, likes, comments, shares and saved are available for all ten selected posts. Follows is available for four feed/carousel posts but API code 100 explicitly rejects it for the six Reels. Missing metrics render a dash, never zero. Media insights are lifetime values, not September-only activity. Actual UTC publication dates span September 7–October 1, 2026; the final supplied URL is the October 1 post and remains in the selection.

The page reads the shared Social snapshot first, falling back to the previously saved local `.state/social/instagram-posts.json` if needed. Page visits do not trigger API refreshes. A daily worker is prepared to fetch once at 08:15 UTC before Testing Insights at 09:15 UTC; it is not active until deployed and enabled. Explicit manual refresh remains available. A failed or rate-limited fetch keeps the previous shared snapshot and update time; no credentials or paging access tokens are saved in the snapshot. See `docs/testing-social-refresh.md` for the schedule and deployment status.


Social Media now shows Organic and Paid rows per selected original post without adding page explanations. Paid traffic comes from the configured Meta ad account, filtered to Instagram placements and actual delivered ads since the first selected post. Creative source/effective Instagram media IDs and exact permalink shortcodes link promoted copies to original posts. Graph batch GET reads creative metadata; publisher-platform filtering excludes Facebook. Each post uses one account-level Insights query filtered to all linked ad IDs so Meta deduplicates paid reach across ads. Paid video Views use video_play_actions; image/carousel Views use impressions. Missing attributed follows remain unavailable. Organic and paid reach are not added together. Full pagination and six-hour version-2 snapshots prevent repeated ad scans and invalidate prior organic-only caches.

Concluded archive filtering is separate from content-performance eligibility. Non-content experiments retain their archive entries, lifetime snapshots and supporting materials. Legacy /concluded/google links still resolve to Google Search.

### Testing metric display order

All channel testing tables and creative details use this priority for metrics already displayed: Spend → Revenue → ROAS → #Purchase → CVR → Impressions → Clicks → CTR → CPA → AOV. Other existing metrics follow in their previous relative order. Never add a metric merely to fill this sequence. `lib/testing-metric-order.ts` supplies the shared ordering; EDM's existing Click Rate is treated as CTR, and Total Revenue as Revenue. Existing purchase counts are labeled #Purchase. Social metrics without a matching priority retain their original order.

### Google warehouse-first data refresh

Google Search and Demand Gen ad-day metrics and PMax asset-day metrics read the dedicated Google warehouse before Google Ads API. Only successful, account-scoped `etl_run_log` queries with an unfiltered explicit `segments.date BETWEEN` window prove coverage (including legitimate no-delivery days). Adjacent windows merge; API reads cover only missing windows. Recent attribution requires a successful extraction within 36 hours; the current partial day always remains an API supplement. Database errors fall back to the same scoped API query without publishing zeros. Testing inventory/labels and creative discovery remain API-backed because the warehouse does not contain the required complete labels/Demand Gen content. PMax asset-group totals and root listing-group product performance remain API-backed; `fact_product_daily` is not an equivalent asset-group root report.

The three Google refresh buttons now refresh all three channels in one batch. All upstream work must succeed before a transaction publishes the three payloads with one completion timestamp. Any failure leaves all previous payloads/timestamps intact. API quota protection retains snapshot reuse, serial Google discovery and the 30-second shared refresh limit. Meta and Reddit initialize a missing period snapshot from a complete uncached API read; later page reads reuse it, and explicit refresh replaces it only on complete success. API cache read time is never presented as a successful source refresh.

### Daily refresh and website snapshot cache (Eastern time)

- Meta, Reddit, EDM and Social refresh at **04:00 America/New_York**. Only failed scopes retry at **05:00**, once. Successful scopes are never refetched by that retry. Exact Last Week snapshots are reused for their fixed range; WTD/MTD and Reddit/Social lifetime snapshots update daily.
- Google snapshots refresh together at **05:15** after confirming today's Google warehouse ad/asset sync jobs succeeded. Google Ads Scripts are managed separately in the marketing account; the user set the script window to 01:00–02:00 Pacific (04:00–05:00 Eastern).
- AI starts at **05:20**, only after today's collection and Google snapshots completed. It reads those saved Dashboard snapshots. The existing English-first / saved Chinese translation workflow and Tuesday-only Last Week analysis policy remain.
- Failure after the 05:00 retry (or Google/AI's later scheduled attempt) DMs Bintian using the existing AI Bluevua Slack bot, including sanitized scope/reason. Notification delivery can retry without repeating exhausted API calls. No tokens enter Git. Failed refreshes retain previous snapshots, timestamps and AI results.
- Raw snapshots are persisted in shared Postgres `testing_data_snapshots`. Server pages reuse a bounded, positive-only five-minute process cache; successful publication updates/invalidates it immediately. The uncached status read refreshes that process cache before the UI refreshes its page. An unavailable database can retain a stale cached snapshot instead of zero filling. This cache is shared data, never personal Ask AI conversations.
- Disable the superseded `emw3-testing-social-refresh.timer` when enabling the unified collector to avoid duplicate calls. Install the three service/timer pairs under `ops/` and reload systemd; do not run today's AI again merely to install a schedule.

Google daily performance snapshots end on the latest complete calendar day, matching selectable reporting periods, so an unused partial current day does not trigger an additional Google API metrics request. Current inventory/status discovery still occurs during the refresh.
